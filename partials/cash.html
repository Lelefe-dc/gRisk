Basic Health Cash Plan

Hospital cash support when you need it

Choose between Basic Saver and Basic Plus hospital cash cover. Normal illness has a three-day deferred period; accidental hospitalisation has zero deferred days.

  • Payment for normal illness starts on the fourth day of hospitalisation.
  • Maximum 30 hospitalisation days per single event.
  • Maximum 60 hospitalisation days per year.
  • Hospitalisation should be notified by the end of the third day.
